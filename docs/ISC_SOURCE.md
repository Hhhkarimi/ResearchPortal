# ISC roster authority

The project roster is the 115 public/government institutions included in the ISC national classification for 1402–1401.

The publicly accessible announcement states that 115 public and 21 non-governmental universities were evaluated, with the public institutions classified into:

- 69 comprehensive
- 24 industrial
- 4 art
- 4 agricultural-sciences
- 4 subsystem
- 10 executive-agency institutions

The results were reported as approved in the 980th Higher Education Expansion Council meeting on 24 Farvardin 1404.

`data/isc/institutions.json` is the locked roster used by validation. The repository intentionally excludes the 21 non-governmental institutions and medical-sciences universities.

Source metadata and public reproduction URL are recorded in `data/isc/source.json`.
