## Methodology and controlled taxonomy

The inventory and ordering are taken exactly from `data/isc/institutions.json`. On 11 August 2026, each institution received two independent web-search passes: one for its Research & Technology Vice-Presidency/office and its units, and one for document indexes and direct files using Persian terms for forms, regulations, bylaws, procedures and guidelines. Existing package URLs were treated only as discovery leads. A URL entered in this table had to be on the institution's own official domain or a verified official subdomain. Social channels, news agencies, mirrors, aggregators and another university's repost were excluded.

A value of `—` means unresolved in this public-web pass, not absent. Restricted, unindexed, CAPTCHA-protected, timed-out, TLS-failing and currently unavailable pages were not converted into negative claims. Where a known official attempted URL could be stated without guessing, it is retained in the relevant URL cell; otherwise the cell is `—`.

The controlled labels embedded at the beginning of every `directDocuments` item are: `regulation/bylaw`, `procedure/guideline`, `form/template`, `policy/circular`, `research ethics`, `grants/funding`, `publications/journals`, `laboratory`, `industry/technology/IP`, `postgraduate/research affairs`, and `other`. Each item keeps the required machine form `[taxonomy] title :: URL`; multiple items and all URL blocks use semicolons.

IT evidence is intentionally conservative: an IT page is recorded only when an official page or catalog places IT within the relevant organizational structure. Mere existence of a university IT center is insufficient. Homepage visibility alone likewise does not prove a laboratory, document center, system, or subordinate unit.

## Re-audit limitations

Search-engine indexing is incomplete for many Iranian university portals, and several official domains intermittently returned access, TLS, timeout or crawler restrictions during this pass. Direct-document discovery therefore remains lower than portal discovery. This report is an auditable discovery snapshot, not proof that unresolved documents or units do not exist, and unresolved values must not be scored as zero in RTPMI.
