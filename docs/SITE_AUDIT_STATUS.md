# Site-by-site Research Portal Audit Status

The Audit Register contains one row for every one of the 115 public ISC institutions.

## `direct-official`
A direct official Research & Technology Vice-Presidency portal/surface is recorded. These records can proceed to deep extraction of organizational units, systems and documents. Numeric RTPMI still requires the evidence/confidence gate.

## `official-reference`
Official university-domain or official university communication evidence confirms the research/technology function, but a dedicated direct portal/surface has not been resolved with enough confidence. No numeric score is produced.

## `unresolved-public-portal`
The institution was checked in the roster, but the current evidence snapshot does not establish a direct public research portal. This is not proof of absence and is never converted to zero.

## Deep extraction fields
For direct portals the package stores and expands, when verifiable:
- research-management structure
- library/document centre
- central/research laboratories
- SHAA/national laboratory-network relationship
- industry/society and technology/TTO/IP
- publications/journals/scientometrics
- IT only with organizational evidence
- research systems/services
- regulations, guidelines, procedures and forms with source links
